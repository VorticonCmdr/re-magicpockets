Magic Pockets level viewer

Tile value V at grid (row, col) draws atlas tile V-1 from the selected blocksN.pak sheet (32x16 px/tile, 1-based on-disk indices) -- see docs/formats/pak.md and docs/formats/pap.md. Hover a tile to see its raw index. Entity markers (colored dots) are enemy/item/trigger placements decoded from the level-script .pin files -- see docs/formats/pin-level-script.md. A meaningful fraction have unresolved (often negative or off-map) coordinates -- shown as-is, not clipped.
