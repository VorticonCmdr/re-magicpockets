'''Magic Pockets''' uses several compact binary formats for its DOS graphics, maps, level events and sound. These pages describe the DOS asset collection analysed by the re-magicpockets project.

Sixty files in the supplied <tt>data</tt> directory have an [[Magic Pockets RNC Compression|RNC ProPack method 1]] wrapper. Decompress these before interpreting the formats below. <tt>install.txt</tt> and <tt>pockets.ini</tt> are uncompressed text; the logo resources embedded in the unpacked logo executable use a separate archive and LZHUF compression.

The filename matters as much as the extension. In particular, <tt>.pin</tt> identifies several unrelated formats, and <tt>.pak</tt> contains three different fixed-size graphics layouts.

== Notes ==

* Sizes and offsets on the format pages refer to decompressed data unless explicitly stated otherwise.
* Big-endian level records and picture words coexist with little-endian sound tables and 8086 executable code. There is no universal byte order.
* Sprite palettes, animation descriptors and tile collision classifications are held in the executable, rather than in the sprite or map files.
* The RE notes retain older hypotheses alongside later corrections. The image and music pages use the newer DEGAS and dictionary-MIDI findings. Unknown layouts remain marked as unknown.
* Viewing or exporting a format does not imply that an editor or a compressor is available. The capability flags above indicate partial support.
