Magic Pockets port — Phase 7 checkpoint

Audio: pick your local, unmodified data/ folder, then click "enable audio" (browsers block sound until a user gesture). Sound effects (block breaks, pickups, damage, death, bonus spawns, reaching the exit) and looping level music now play — real MIDI cues extracted from adlib.pin's 78-cue sound-effect bank and roland2.pin's embedded song (docs/formats/pin.md), played through a small Web Audio oscillator-per-note synth (web/audio/synth.js, adapted from tools/midi-player.html) — NOT a real OPL2/MT-32 hardware emulation, so timbre won't match the original AdLib/Roland sound. The event→sound-ID mapping itself is unconfirmed/arbitrary (web/audio/sound-manager.js) — which of the 78 real cues corresponds to which real gameplay event isn't decoded anywhere upstream, so this port just gives each event its own distinct real cue rather than claiming a verified mapping.
